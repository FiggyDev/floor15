# Sponsorship — In-World Only

No banners. Every ad unit is a joke the show wants to tell anyway: the vending machine, the
coffee cup, the elevator screen, the lobby billboard, The Deck (an agent presents your actual
slides, wrong), Client of the Week, Party Night, Season Presenting.

health claims, or politics · you approve the setup, the show owns the punchline · Linda's veto
is real, final, and happens on camera — rejections become a segment · one free make-good if a
beat misfires.
